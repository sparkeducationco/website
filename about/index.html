About Spark

Security researchers, not spectators.

We build browser-native detection for the way students actually use the web—and the way modern bypasses actually operate.

OUR ORIGIN

We build from the browser outward.

Spark is built by security researchers who study the proxy runtimes and browser behavior that slip past domain-only filters. Our detector runs locally in the browser, compiled from Rust to WebAssembly.

OUR MISSION

Keep students safe—with evidence that keeps up.

Students deserve an open, useful internet. Districts deserve a clear, explainable decision when a proxy or browser game crosses the line—without exporting page-level browsing telemetry.
