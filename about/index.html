About Spark Education Technology

Built for the browser
students actually use.

Spark helps K–12 districts keep up with browser-based bypasses and games through detection that starts where the behavior happens: in the browser.

01

Why Spark exists

School filtering needs a more complete signal.

Traditional filters were designed around destinations. But modern bypasses use browser runtimes, frames, scripts, and changing infrastructure to hide the behavior that matters.

Spark was founded by security researchers with direct experience studying and building the technologies schools need to recognize. We built the product around that practical understanding—not around a larger domain list.

How we build

Technology with district realities in mind.

01

Browser-native

Detection runs locally in the managed browser, where the relevant runtime and page signals are available.

02

Operationally clear

District teams get decisions grounded in observable behavior and managed policy—not a black-box score.

03

Privacy-aware

Spark is designed to make local detection decisions without using page-level browsing telemetry as a product input.
