Why Spark exists

The web changed.
School filtering should, too.

Students encounter a fast-moving web. District teams deserve protection designed with that reality in mind.

Our team has built and maintained tools used to bypass existing school filtering software. That hands-on experience gave us a clear view of where traditional filters fall short—and informed our approach to building protection that keeps pace with the web.
